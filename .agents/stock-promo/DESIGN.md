## Style Prompt

Institutional financial editorial on midnight navy, large Vietnamese typography and a restrained market grid. Red urgency gives way to emerald discipline and metallic gold confidence.

#060d19 canvas; #f0f4fa ink; #ff556c urgency; #39edaf discipline; #e8c575 future.

## What NOT to Do

No landscape framing, no fake performance percentages, no text beneath the chart, no unrelated logos.
