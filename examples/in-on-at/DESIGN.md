# Visual identity

## Style Prompt

Explosive kinetic typography with a minimal faceless 2D stick figure and precise spatial diagrams. Warm cream editorial canvas, oversized heavy sans type, navy strokes, mint containment, golden surface contact, coral map location. Bounce with purpose; leave readable holds.

## Colors

- #F7F3E8 canvas
- #142C3B ink
- #52D6B0 IN containment
- #FFD34E ON contact
- #F26655 AT location

## Typography

DejaVu Sans Bold display; DejaVu Sans supporting labels. Bundled local font, no remote requests.

## What NOT to Do

- Never draw eyes, nose, mouth or facial interiors. Head is one unfilled stroked circle.
- Never confuse ON with floating above the surface.
- Never imply AT means inside a location.
- No tiny essential text, gradients, stock footage, or decorative emoji faces on the character.
